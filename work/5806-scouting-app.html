5806 Scouting

Developer · 2026

Match-scouting web app for FRC Team 5806 — collect match data from the stands, then rank teams for alliance selection.

TypeScript · Next.js · React · FRC

  • Source ↗

Problem

At a competition, alliance selection depends on what you know about every other robot in the building. Paper scouting sheets get lost, and spreadsheets built on the day are slow to fill and slower to trust.

Approach

A phone-first web app for the people in the stands: one screen per match, big buttons, works with bad venue Wi-Fi. Data lands in one place so the strategy lead can sort and compare teams on the numbers that matter for this year's game.

Result

Open source on GitHub. Used by Team 5806 during the 2026 season.
