## Problem

FIRST Robotics gives a 35-person team roughly six weeks to take a brand-new game from kickoff to a
competition-ready robot. Every mechanism has to be designed, cut, assembled and tested against a
deadline that does not move, by people whose experience ranges from first-season freshmen to
graduating seniors.

## Approach

As Design, Build & Test Lead I owned robot mechanisms end to end: CAD in Autodesk Fusion 360,
manufacturing drawings, cutting plate on the waterjet, machining on the 2-axis mill, and assembly.
Each mechanism went through prototype → test → revision loops instead of a single big build, so
problems showed up on a bench, not on the field.

As Co-Captain this season I coordinate the design, fabrication and programming subteams: build
schedules, task assignment, mentoring newer members on CAD and safe shop practice, and keeping
mentors and students talking to each other.
