## Problem

At a competition, alliance selection depends on what you know about every other robot in the
building. Paper scouting sheets get lost, and spreadsheets built on the day are slow to fill and
slower to trust.

## Approach

A phone-first web app for the people in the stands: one screen per match, big buttons, works with
bad venue Wi-Fi. Data lands in one place so the strategy lead can sort and compare teams on the
numbers that matter for this year's game.
