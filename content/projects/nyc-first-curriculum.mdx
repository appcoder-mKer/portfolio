## Problem

Eight-year-olds don't want a lecture about circuits. They want the boat to move. The job was to turn
real electronics and engineering concepts into two-hour sessions where every student leaves with
something that works.

## Approach

Each lesson wrapped one concept inside one build: motors and switches inside a motorized boat,
control inside an RC car, 2D design and tolerances inside a laser-cut keychain. I ran the laser
cutter and electronics stations, trained students on equipment and safety, and applied the same
design–test–iterate loop to a facility improvement project for the space itself.
